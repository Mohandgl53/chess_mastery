PHASE 8 FEATURE 2 — CONTEXT-AWARE COACH
==================================================

The Coach should understand what is happening on the board.

Example:

Player makes a move that Stockfish identifies as a mistake.

The Coach should be able to explain:

1. What the player played
2. What the stronger move was
3. Why the move was problematic
4. What tactical/positional idea was missed
5. How this connects to the player's known weaknesses
6. What the player should practice

Example structure:

"You played Bxf7+.

The stronger move was Nf3.

The issue is that your bishop exchange allows...

This connects to your recent difficulty with piece coordination.

A useful next step is to practice..."

Every chess fact must originate from trusted systems.
